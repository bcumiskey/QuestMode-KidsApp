# Quest & Grow — First-Time Experience: Full Simulation

## Overview

This document walks through the COMPLETE first-time experience for each child. Every screen. Every tap. Every moment of wonder. This is the most important 5 minutes of the entire app — if we lose them here, we lose them forever.

**Goal:** By the end of onboarding, every child should:
1. Have a creature they already love
2. Have a base that feels like THEIRS
3. Understand that doing stuff = cool things happen
4. Be EXCITED to come back tomorrow
5. Have already completed their first quest with maximum celebration

---

## Technical Requirements for Onboarding

1. **Track onboarding state** — `onboardingComplete: boolean`, `onboardingStep: number`
2. **Rigged first spin** — First bonus wheel is NOT random, guarantees great outcome
3. **Free starter pack** — Creature, building, resources granted on completion
4. **Mascot animations** — Need idle, talk, celebrate, point states
5. **Age-tier rendering** — Same flow, different presentation
6. **Skip option** — For older kids / returning users (Ann only)
7. **Parent setup first** — Parent creates child profile with age, then child gets onboarding

## First Session Must End With:

✅ Met their mascot (relationship started)
✅ Named a creature (ownership)  
✅ Saw their base (home established)
✅ Completed one quest (learned the loop)
✅ Got a big celebration (felt amazing)
✅ Won something from a spin (luck/excitement)
✅ Started a streak (reason to return)
✅ Has an egg incubating (anticipation)
✅ Knows what to do next (clarity)

If ANY of these are missing, we've failed.
